# Squat

## Proper Form
- Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out.
- Keep your chest up and core braced throughout the movement.
- Initiate the squat by pushing your hips back, as if sitting into a chair.
- Lower until your thighs are at least parallel to the ground.
- Keep your knees tracking over your toes — do not let them cave inward.
- Drive through your heels to stand back up.

## Common Mistakes
- Knees caving inward (valgus collapse): Strengthen glutes and focus on pushing knees outward.
- Leaning too far forward: This often indicates tight hip flexors or weak core. Keep the chest up.
- Not hitting depth: Work on ankle and hip mobility. Use a box or bench as a depth target.
- Rising on toes: Focus on keeping weight distributed across the whole foot.
- Lower back rounding (butt wink): Improve hip mobility and stop at the depth where your pelvis starts to tuck.

## Muscles Worked
Primary: Quadriceps, Glutes. Secondary: Hamstrings, Core, Erector Spinae.

## Breathing
Inhale at the top, brace your core. Exhale as you push up from the bottom.

# Push-up

## Proper Form
- Start in a plank position with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
- Keep your body in a straight line from head to heels.
- Lower your chest to the ground by bending your elbows.
- Elbows should be at roughly a 45-degree angle to your body (not flared out at 90 degrees).
- Push back up to full arm extension.

## Common Mistakes
- Hips sagging: Engage your core and glutes to maintain a straight line.
- Hips piking up: Lower your hips until your body forms a plank.
- Flaring elbows at 90 degrees: Tuck them closer to 45 degrees to protect your shoulders.
- Not going low enough: Aim for your chest to touch or nearly touch the ground.
- Head dropping: Keep your neck neutral by looking at the floor a foot ahead of your hands.

## Muscles Worked
Primary: Chest (Pectorals), Triceps. Secondary: Anterior Deltoids, Core.

## Breathing
Inhale on the way down, exhale on the way up.

# Bicep Curl

## Proper Form
- Stand upright with a dumbbell in each hand, arms fully extended, palms facing forward.
- Keep your elbows pinned to your sides throughout the movement.
- Curl the weights upward by contracting your biceps.
- Squeeze at the top, then slowly lower back to the starting position.
- Control the movement in both directions — do not use momentum.

## Common Mistakes
- Swinging the torso: Using body momentum means the weight is too heavy. Reduce weight and isolate the bicep.
- Elbow drifting forward: Your elbows should stay at your sides. Drifting forward shifts the load to the front deltoid.
- Incomplete range of motion: Fully extend at the bottom and fully contract at the top.
- Wrist curling: Keep wrists neutral and straight.

## Muscles Worked
Primary: Biceps Brachii. Secondary: Brachialis, Brachioradialis, Forearm muscles.

## Breathing
Exhale as you curl up, inhale as you lower.

# Shoulder Press

## Proper Form
- Sit or stand with dumbbells at shoulder height, palms facing forward.
- Press the weights overhead until your arms are fully extended.
- Lower the weights back to shoulder height in a controlled manner.
- Keep your core braced and avoid arching your lower back.

## Common Mistakes
- Excessive lower back arch: This usually means the weight is too heavy. Brace your core or use a bench with back support.
- Not achieving full lockout: Extend arms fully at the top for complete range of motion.
- Flaring elbows too wide: Keep elbows slightly in front of your body.
- Using legs to push: Unless doing a push press, keep your legs still.

## Muscles Worked
Primary: Anterior and Lateral Deltoids, Triceps. Secondary: Upper Chest, Trapezius, Core.

## Breathing
Exhale as you press up, inhale as you lower.

# Lunges

## Proper Form
- Stand tall with feet hip-width apart.
- Step forward with one leg, lowering your hips until both knees are at roughly 90 degrees.
- Your front knee should track over your toes but not extend far past them.
- Your back knee should hover just above the ground.
- Push through the front heel to return to the starting position.
- Keep your torso upright throughout the movement.

## Common Mistakes
- Front knee going past toes excessively: Take a longer step forward.
- Losing balance: Keep feet hip-width apart (not in a straight line) and engage your core.
- Torso leaning forward: Keep your chest up and shoulders back.
- Back knee slamming the ground: Control the descent — hover just above the floor.

## Muscles Worked
Primary: Quadriceps, Glutes. Secondary: Hamstrings, Calves, Core.

## Breathing
Inhale as you step forward and lower, exhale as you push back up.
