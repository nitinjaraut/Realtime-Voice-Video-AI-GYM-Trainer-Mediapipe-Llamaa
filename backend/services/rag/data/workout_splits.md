# Workout Splits and Training Routines

## 1. Push-Pull-Legs (PPL) Split
The Push-Pull-Legs (PPL) split is one of the most effective and popular training routines for muscle hypertrophy and strength. It groups muscles based on their movement patterns.

### How PPL Works:
- **Push Day**: Targets chest, front/side shoulders, and triceps. Exercises: Bench press, incline dumbbell press, overhead shoulder press, lateral raises, triceps pushdowns, and dips.
- **Pull Day**: Targets upper back, lats, rear shoulders, and biceps. Exercises: Barbell deadlifts, pull-ups, barbell rows, lat pulldowns, face pulls, and bicep curls.
- **Leg Day**: Targets quads, hamstrings, glutes, and calves. Exercises: Barbell squats, Romanian deadlifts, leg press, lunges, leg curls, and calf raises.

### Common PPL Schedules:
- **3-Day Split (Beginner / Intermediate)**: Monday (Push), Wednesday (Pull), Friday (Legs). Ideal for busy schedules and recovery.
- **6-Day Split (Advanced)**: Push / Pull / Legs / Rest / Push / Pull / Legs / Rest. Allows hitting every muscle group twice every 7-8 days.

## 2. Upper / Lower Split
The Upper/Lower split alternates between upper-body and lower-body training days. It is often considered the gold standard for natural lifters looking to maximize muscle growth.

### Schedule (4 Days per Week):
- **Monday**: Upper Body A (Chest, Back focus, Shoulders, Arms)
- **Tuesday**: Lower Body A (Squat focus, Quads, Calves, Core)
- **Wednesday**: Rest / Active Recovery
- **Thursday**: Upper Body B (Shoulders, Upper Back focus, Arms)
- **Friday**: Lower Body B (Deadlift / Hamstring focus, Glutes, Calves)
- **Saturday / Sunday**: Rest

### Advantages:
- Hits each muscle twice per week with 48-72 hours of recovery between sessions.
- Easily fits a standard work-week schedule (Monday-Friday).

---

## 3. Full-Body Routine
A Full-Body split trains all major muscle groups in a single workout, typically performed 3 non-consecutive days per week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).

### Advantages:
- Best for beginners to practice compound movement patterns frequently.
- High training efficiency: missed workouts are easy to compensate for.
- Excellent calorie expenditure and cardiovascular conditioning.

---

## 4. "Bro Split" (Body Part Split)
The traditional body part split dedicates each workout day to a single muscle group.

### Schedule (5 Days per Week):
- Day 1: Chest
- Day 2: Back
- Day 3: Shoulders
- Day 4: Legs
- Day 5: Arms (Biceps & Triceps)

### Advantages & Considerations:
- Allows high volume per muscle in one workout session.
- Each muscle is trained once per week, which may offer slower progression than training muscles 2x per week.

---

## 5. Arnold Split
Popularized by Arnold Schwarzenegger, this split pairs opposing muscle groups (antagonistic supersets).
- **Day 1**: Chest & Back
- **Day 2**: Shoulders & Arms (Biceps + Triceps)
- **Day 3**: Legs & Lower Back
- Repeated twice weekly for 6 days total.

---

## Choosing the Right Split
1. **Choose based on schedule consistency**: A 3-day full body routine done consistently beats an abandoned 6-day PPL routine.
2. **Frequency matters**: Natural lifters generally build more muscle when training muscles 2 times per week rather than once per week.
3. **Progressive overload**: Track your weights and reps each week regardless of the split you choose.
