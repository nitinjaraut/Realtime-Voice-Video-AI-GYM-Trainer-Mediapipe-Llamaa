# Nutrition Basics for Fitness

## Pre-Workout Nutrition
- Eat a meal 2-3 hours before your workout, or a snack 30-60 minutes before.
- Focus on carbohydrates for energy: oatmeal, banana, toast with peanut butter.
- Include moderate protein: Greek yogurt, protein shake, eggs.
- Keep fat intake low in the immediate pre-workout meal as it slows digestion.
- Stay hydrated — drink 500ml of water 2 hours before exercising.

## Post-Workout Nutrition
- Eat within 30-60 minutes after your workout for optimal recovery.
- Prioritize protein to support muscle repair: 20-40g of protein (chicken breast, protein shake, eggs, cottage cheese).
- Include carbohydrates to replenish glycogen: rice, potatoes, fruit, whole grain bread.
- A simple post-workout meal example: grilled chicken with rice and vegetables.
- Rehydrate with water or electrolyte drinks after sweating heavily.

## Daily Protein Requirements
- For general fitness: 1.2-1.6g of protein per kilogram of body weight per day.
- For muscle building: 1.6-2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight per day.
- Good protein sources: chicken, fish, eggs, Greek yogurt, lentils, tofu, whey protein.

## Hydration
- Drink at least 2-3 liters of water per day.
- During exercise, drink 200-300ml every 15-20 minutes.
- If exercising for more than 60 minutes, consider electrolyte drinks.
- Signs of dehydration: dark urine, headache, dizziness, fatigue.

## General Guidelines
- Eat a balanced diet with all three macronutrients: protein, carbs, and fats.
- Do not skip meals, especially breakfast — it fuels your morning training.
- Avoid excessive sugar and processed foods.
- Whole foods are generally better than supplements, but protein shakes are convenient if you struggle to meet protein targets.

## Supplements (General, Not Medical Advice)
- Creatine monohydrate (3-5g/day) is one of the most researched and safe supplements for strength training.
- Caffeine (150-300mg) can improve workout performance when taken 30-60 minutes before exercise.
- Multivitamins can fill nutritional gaps but should not replace a balanced diet.
- Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any supplement.
